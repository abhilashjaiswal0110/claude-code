# Structured Interview Framework (Sample)

## Interview Structure

### Round 1: Technical Screening (45 min)
- Coding exercise (25 min)
- System design discussion (15 min)
- Q&A (5 min)

### Round 2: Technical Deep Dive (60 min)
- Architecture discussion (20 min)
- Problem-solving scenarios (25 min)
- Code review exercise (15 min)

### Round 3: Behavioral & Culture (45 min)
- STAR method questions (30 min)
- Culture fit discussion (10 min)
- Candidate questions (5 min)

### Round 4: Hiring Manager (30 min)
- Career goals and alignment (15 min)
- Team dynamics discussion (10 min)
- Logistics and next steps (5 min)

## Evaluation Criteria
- Technical competency (1-5)
- Problem-solving ability (1-5)
- Communication skills (1-5)
- Cultural alignment (1-5)
- Growth potential (1-5)

## Bias Prevention
- Use standardized questions for all candidates
- Score immediately after each interview
- Avoid discussing candidates between rounds
- Focus on demonstrated competencies, not demographics

*Note: This is a sample framework for demonstration purposes.*
