# Recruitment Compliance Guidelines (Sample)

## Equal Opportunity
- All job descriptions must include an equal opportunity statement
- Selection criteria must be based solely on job-related qualifications
- Reasonable accommodations must be offered for candidates with disabilities

## Inclusive Language
- Avoid gendered language (he/she → they, manpower → workforce)
- Remove age-indicative language (young, energetic, digital native)
- Avoid culturally biased references
- Use "required" vs "preferred" clearly to encourage diverse applicants

## Data Protection
- Candidate data must be handled per GDPR requirements
- Retain candidate data only for the stated recruitment period
- Provide candidates with data access and deletion rights
- Use anonymized data for reporting and analytics

## Interview Standards
- Structured interviews with standardized questions
- Documented evaluation criteria applied consistently
- Panel diversity in interview committees
- Reasonable accommodations for all interview formats

## Offer Process
- Offers must be based on market data and internal equity
- No salary history inquiries (where legally restricted)
- Clear articulation of total compensation package
- Written offer with defined acceptance timeline
