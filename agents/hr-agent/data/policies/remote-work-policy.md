# Remote Work & Hybrid Policy (Sample)

## Eligibility
- All roles classified as "hybrid-eligible" or "remote-eligible"
- Minimum 6 months of employment for full remote work
- Manager approval required

## Hybrid Work Model
- Default: 3 days in-office, 2 days remote per week
- Core in-office days may be designated by team leads
- Flexible scheduling within core hours (10:00 AM - 4:00 PM local time)

## Equipment & Setup
- Company-provided laptop and peripherals
- Monthly internet allowance for remote workers
- Ergonomic assessment available upon request

## Communication Expectations
- Available on company messaging during core hours
- Camera-on policy for key meetings (team standups, client calls)
- Response time SLA: 2 hours during business hours

## Security Requirements
- VPN required for all remote access
- Company-approved devices only
- No public Wi-Fi for sensitive work without VPN
