# Leave Policy (Sample)

## Annual Leave
- All full-time employees are entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year
- Leave must be approved by the reporting manager at least 5 business days in advance
- Unused leave can be carried forward up to 5 days to the next calendar year
- Leave encashment is available for up to 10 days at year-end (subject to manager approval)

## Sick Leave
- 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year
- Medical certificate required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days
- Unused sick leave cannot be carried forward or encashed

## Parental Leave
- Maternity leave: 26 weeks (as per applicable law)
- Paternity leave: 10 working days
- Adoption leave: As per applicable law

## Other Leave Types
- Bereavement leave: 5 days for immediate family
- Marriage leave: 5 days (once during employment)
- Exam leave: Up to 5 days per year (with documentation)
