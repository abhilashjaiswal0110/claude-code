# Employee Benefits Overview (Sample)

## Health & Insurance
- Medical insurance: Employee + spouse + 2 children
- Term life insurance: 3x annual CTC
- Personal accident cover: 2x annual CTC
- Dental and vision coverage (optional add-on)

## Retirement Benefits
- Provident Fund (PF): As per statutory requirements
- Gratuity: As per applicable law
- NPS: Voluntary with company matching up to 5%

## Wellness Programs
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP): 24/7 confidential counseling
- Annual health check-up (fully covered)
- Gym/fitness reimbursement: Up to INR 12,000/year
- Mental health days: 2 additional days per year

## Learning & Development
- Annual learning budget: INR 50,000 per employee
- Certification reimbursement (pre-approved certifications)
- Internal mentorship programs
- LinkedIn Learning access for all employees

## Other Benefits
- Meal allowance / cafeteria subsidy
- Transportation allowance
- Employee referral bonus program
- Long service awards (5, 10, 15, 20 years)
