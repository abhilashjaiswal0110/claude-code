# General HR FAQs (Sample)

## Q: How do I apply for leave?
A: Submit leave requests through the HR portal (MyHR). Navigate to Time & Attendance > Leave Request. Select the leave type, dates, and submit for manager approval.

## Q: What is the notice period?
A: Standard notice period is 90 days for all confirmed employees. During probation, the notice period is 30 days.

## Q: How do I update my personal information?
A: Update personal details (address, emergency contacts, bank details) through the MyHR portal under Profile > Personal Information.

## Q: When is the annual performance review?
A: Performance reviews are conducted annually in Q1 (January-March). Mid-year check-ins occur in July-August.

## Q: How do I raise a grievance?
A: Grievances can be raised through: (1) Direct manager, (2) HR Business Partner, (3) Ethics hotline (anonymous), (4) POSH committee for harassment-related concerns.

## Q: What is the dress code?
A: Business casual on regular days. Formal attire for client meetings and events. Casual Fridays (team-dependent).
